Morsu — a Walrus blob explorer
==============================

You are reading a real blob stored on Walrus mainnet.

Walrus is decentralized storage: this file was erasure-coded into slivers,
spread across a committee of storage nodes, and certified on Sui. No single
server holds it, and no single server can take it down.

Morsu reads blobs like this one from a public aggregator and previews them
in your browser. Paste any Walrus Blob ID into the search box to try your own.
